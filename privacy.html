Privacy Policy

App: Claude Personal Google
Owner and sole user: Moshiko Demri (moshikodemri@gmail.com)
Last updated: November 25, 2026

What the app does

The app is a private integration used only by its owner. It runs locally on the owner's computer and lets the owner's Claude Desktop app read and act on the owner's own Google account data.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app may access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides and Google Contacts data in the owner's account.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to carry out requests made by the owner.
  • When the owner asks Claude to work with this data, the relevant content is processed by the owner's Claude (Anthropic) account to fulfil that request.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer.
  • The app does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone other than as described above.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
